# MPA 0.5.31 — Your camera roll and one People library

Candidate; not published until final validation completes.

- Camera photos and other readable device media join Photos automatically after Android access is granted. Device originals are opened directly; bounded video posters do not copy the full clip.
- Back up one photo or a selection to Google Drive explicitly. Saved jobs keep their captured destination, failed preparations remain retryable, and only verified Drive copies receive the compact badge. Camera originals remain protected.
- Imported names and reviewed faces share People cards, counts, Rename, Merge and corrections. Names already retained from Takeout are indexed without reimporting successful photos. Future imports reuse merged aliases. Optional recognition offers uncertain matches for confirmation.
- Settings/account and person/face menus use rounded groups and focused pages. Naming drafts survive rotation. Gallery arrivals and preview changes retain stable media positions, with an explicit New photos action.
- Portable annotations preserve names-only memberships, aliases, exclusions and removed-name markers alongside unchanged Takeout metadata. Room 11 is additive; existing originals, identities, jobs, consents and checkpoints are retained.

After updating, open the account/source icon → **Photos on this device** to grant Android access. To combine two existing cards for the same person, open either card's menu → **Merge people**. New device media is not uploaded automatically by granting browsing access.

Final source, test/diagnostic evidence, APK checksum and signing certificate will be recorded before publication. Physical S23 Ultra/live Google/human accessibility acceptance remain pending; the earlier JPEG crash is unreproduced.
