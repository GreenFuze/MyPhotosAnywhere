## Floating date refinement

The owner's Google Photos screenshot shows the date floating above the grid. MPA now centers its date action in a rounded pill over the photo viewport and removes the calendar icon. It shows the first intersecting current-query photo's month/year. The full device-local date is available to accessibility services without repeated live announcements. Unknown dates remain unknown instead of borrowing a later photo's date. No location or metadata is invented.

Tap the pill to open the existing month/year navigator. Inline semantic date headings, the exact-day picker, saved 2/3/4-column density, selection, pixel anchors, paging and viewer behavior remain. The pill is hidden during selection/restoration, stays clear of bottom navigation, wraps at enlarged font sizes and remains centered in RTL. This is a focused refinement of the completed UX batches.

No media metadata, schema, original-download pipeline, worker, import checkpoint, destination or consent contract changes. The unused calendar vector was removed rather than suppressed in lint.
