# MyPhotosAnywhere 0.5.11

[Download MPA-0.5.11-debug.apk](https://github.com/GreenFuze/MyPhotosAnywhere/releases/download/v0.5.11/MPA-0.5.11-debug.apk).

Install over the existing MPA app. Package `com.myphotosanywhere.mpa`, the established signing certificate and Room schema 9 are preserved. Uninstalling or clearing app data is not required.

## Changes

- Drag a photo down at fit zoom, or tap Details, to open a scrollable Photo Details sheet over the retained viewer. Close or Back returns to the same photo; rotation preserves context.
- Compact date/filename viewer header and Share, Edit and Favorite actions. Horizontal paging, pinch zoom and zoomed panning retain their gesture ownership.
- Details shows source and real original availability, retaining existing album, archive, offline pin, location, recognized-person correction, export and external-AI workflows. Cloud metadata remains accessible without inventing a local original or losing authorization recovery.
- Search clears keyboard focus when opening a photo and returns to the originating query. Export resolves the saved photo identity directly while preserving original bytes.
- Paging is pinned to stable 3.5.3, including page-retention and separator fixes. No database migration, import/checkpoint change or original decoder/cache rewrite is included.

## Known limits

- **Samsung S23 Ultra acceptance and the reported JPEG crash remain unverified.** Synthetic emulator tests do not establish that crash's cause or resolution. Real-account OAuth/network and Samsung lifecycle behavior still require phone testing.
- At 100,000 indexed items, viewer sequence preparation measured 5.9–29.3 seconds across CI emulators. The selected preview opens before sequence preparation completes; this remains a large-library performance limitation. Exact query, seek, memory and concurrent-import measurements are in BUILD_EVIDENCE.json.
- Existing Android/SDK instrumentation, rendering, missing Google Play services and model-delegate diagnostics remain in captured logcat. Runtime-log review and exact evidence accompany this release.
- Broader selection, Collections, Search and source/activity management redesigns remain later increments. Date/caption editing and new AI functionality are not added. External-AI result import remains manual. Takeout URL input remains absent.
