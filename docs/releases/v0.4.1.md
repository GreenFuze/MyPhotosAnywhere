# MyPhotosAnywhere 0.4.1

Fixes three issues reported while testing 0.4.0 on a Samsung S23 Ultra:

- **Scanning:** Scan now and Pause scans appear at the top of People and Storage. If detection is off, Scan now lets you choose people, objects and connected Drive sources before enabling it.
- **Viewing:** Tapping a photo opens the full-screen viewer directly. Cloud photos show a preview and transfer status until the checksum-verified original is ready. Zoom decodes visible regions from original bytes rather than enlarging a fixed 2,048-pixel image. This bounds memory for large photos and respects EXIF rotation/reflection. Unsupported region formats visibly fall back to a preview. Details, editing, sharing and export remain available from the viewer.
- **Drive deletions:** Deleted or moved-out Drive photos and their downloaded copies disappear from the main gallery after a successful refresh. Cached originals and annotations are preserved; existing copies remain accessible through Offline, favorites and albums. An active overlapping source keeps its shared photo visible. Independently imported originals remain visible even if their backup is deleted. MPA also queues a Drive refresh when returning to the app, respecting each source's network preference.

Download **MPA-0.4.1-debug.apk** and install over 0.4.0. The package and existing signing certificate are preserved; there is no database schema change. This remains a development build for phone testing. Individual pet recognition and stronger human recognition models are unchanged.

Validated with **84 unit/integration tests, seven Android emulator tests and Android lint with zero errors**. The existing signing certificate and Room v5 schema are unchanged. Emulator fixtures verify behavior; they do not establish Samsung camera-format quality or real Drive authorization.

## Retest on the S23 Ultra

1. Open People or Storage: Scan now must be visible without scrolling. Start a scan and check the consent choices, progress and Pause scans.
2. Tap a cached photo: it must immediately open full screen. Pinch into a 12/50/200 MP photo and inspect fine detail; swipe to another photo, then open Details and Edit. Check rotated JPEGs and HEIC files. For a cloud-only photo, check the loading message, Wi-Fi preference and eventual original.
3. Download a test Drive photo, delete it in Drive, return to MPA and choose Refresh Drive. After the refresh succeeds, confirm it disappears from All but the downloaded copy remains in Offline. Test an overlapping connected folder and a separate local import as well.
4. Restart in airplane mode and verify downloaded originals still open. Original export should preserve the file's checksum and metadata.
