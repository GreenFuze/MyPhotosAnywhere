## Justified photo mosaic

The uniform square grid now has an aspect-aware Mosaic layout. Portrait, square and landscape previews receive different widths in aligned chronological rows, with row heights adapting to their shapes. Date headings and the floating date pill remain. Mosaic is the default; **Photos > Filters > Photo layout > Square** restores uniform tiles. Saved density still targets 2/3/4 photos per row. Incomplete days/pages keep their normal footprint, and extreme aspect ratios are bounded for usable tiles.

Geometry comes only from existing orientation-normalized thumbnail headers, read on IO without bitmap pixel buffers, original downloads or metadata rewrites. A per-ViewModel 600-identity LRU freezes shapes within the active paging window; missing previews remain square until a later visit after eviction or process recreation. A bounded retained-row plan changes widths without sorting or deduplicating photos. Rendering pairs immutable identities with their geometry, and viewport lookup uses canonical keys rather than mutable indices.

Square/Mosaic controls have 48dp targets, row packing protects minimum tile targets on narrow windows, and accessibility services receive logical photo columns instead of virtual packing slots. Layout switching restores the canonical anchor and pixel offset. Selection, date navigation, viewer sessions, image decoding, leases, imports and storage contracts remain. Room schema 9 is unchanged.

This is a justified mosaic with aligned rows. It does not reproduce Google's private layout algorithm or occasional portrait tiles spanning multiple rows. Some previews remain cropped, and unknown preview shapes are not invented. There is no new server, recognition feature or paid API.
