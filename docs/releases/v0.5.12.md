# MyPhotosAnywhere 0.5.12

[Download MPA-0.5.12-debug.apk](https://github.com/GreenFuze/MyPhotosAnywhere/releases/download/v0.5.12/MPA-0.5.12-debug.apk).

Install over the existing MPA app. Package `com.myphotosanywhere.mpa`, the established upgrade signer and Room schema 9 are preserved. Uninstalling or clearing app data is not required.

## Changes

- **Swipe up for Photo Details.** Swipe down no longer opens the sheet. The visible Details button, retained viewer, horizontal paging, pinch zoom and zoomed panning remain available.
- Long-press a photo or use its accessible Select action, then tap more thumbnails. Selection uses stable media identities and survives rotation/import updates. Back exits selection; contextual actions replace normal navigation.
- Bulk Favorite/Unfavorite, Add to album, Archive/Restore, Offline and Share actions. Album grids also offer Remove from album, which never deletes an original. Failed items remain selected with per-item explanations. Changed/missing items are not substituted.
- Share prepares verified original bytes under cache leases and provides secure Android content URIs. Failure or cancellation cleans its private staging and shares no subset. Original metadata, including GPS, is preserved when sharing originals.
- Collections now has adaptive thumbnail cards for Favorites, Archive, People, Places, Offline and Edited photos, plus visual albums with existing create/manage/pin actions. Cards retain at most four covers; Offline cards inspect at most 24 candidate paths; Collections retains its scroll position.
- No database migration, import/checkpoint rewrite, original decoder change, automatic recognition or online map activation.

## Known limits and next work

- **Samsung S23 Ultra acceptance and the previously reported JPEG crash remain unverified.** Emulator fixtures do not establish that crash's cause or resolution. Real-account OAuth/network and Samsung lifecycle behavior still need phone validation.
- Unlinked remote-only items require an original download before existing local metadata can be organized. They report that requirement and remain selected; Offline queues the existing verified download. Queued downloads are not claimed as completed/offline available.
- Selection batches are explicitly bounded to 500 items. Drag-range selection and global select-all across incomplete pages are not included. Dedicated Search presentation and source/activity/settings redesigns remain later increments.
- At 100,000 indexed items, viewer sequence preparation measured 8.0–12.8 seconds across CI emulators. The selected preview opens before preparation completes. This remains a large-library performance limitation; detailed query/seek/memory/import measurements are attached.
- Existing Android/SDK instrumentation, rendering, missing Google Play services and model-delegate diagnostics remain in captured logcat. Full diagnostics and runtime-log review accompany the evidence.
- External-AI result import remains manual. Takeout URL input remains absent. Recognition/map consent and data/import protections are unchanged.
