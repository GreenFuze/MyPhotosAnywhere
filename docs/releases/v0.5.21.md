# MPA 0.5.21 — Continuous photo grid

Photos now flow continuously across calendar days, months and years. Full-width date separators are removed from the shared timeline presentation, letting both Mosaic and Square pack adjacent photos without date-related gaps. The floating month/year capsule remains, with accessible full-date context and **Jump to date**. Photo descriptions and Details retain dates; unknown dates remain explicit.

After a date jump, the capsule keeps the target photo's date while its row remains at the top. It advances as you scroll to another row, even when adjacent tiles cross a month or year boundary.

This implements the owner's refinement to the original permanent-date-header recommendation. Thumbnail descriptions explicitly follow the current device timezone. Chronological ordering, timestamp provenance, filters, paging limits, stable identities, viewer sessions, original availability, storage and imports remain. No metadata rewrite or database migration is included.

The [refinement roadmap](https://github.com/GreenFuze/MyPhotosAnywhere/blob/main/docs/PHOTO_FIRST_REFINEMENT_ROADMAP.md) now places visual Collections in 0.5.22, followed by richer mosaic composition, continuous selection, direct date navigation and management/acceptance polish through 0.5.26.
